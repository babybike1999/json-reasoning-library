# Music Publishing Assistant — Start Here

Source: *Introduction to Music Publishing for Musicians: Business and Creative Perspectives for the New Music Industry* by Bobby Borg and Michael Eames, Rowman & Littlefield Publishing Group, Inc., 2021. Source format: EPUB supplied as a ZIP archive. EPUB SHA-256: `bd33136b7a94b1e5be1eb1147c6b080847b2ba0a94d866fda20fd3277dcdbf67`.

This bundle is a music-publishing reasoning operating system for education, documentation, and issue spotting. It is not legal, tax, accounting, or rights-administration advice.

## First principles

1. Separate the composition from the master recording.
2. Separate ownership, administration, licensing, collection, accounting, and payment.
3. Separate writer share, publisher share, master participation, and cash compensation.
4. Identify the exact right, asset, owner/controller, grant, term, territory, approvals, income split, deductions, reversion and documentation gaps.
5. Do not assume payment automatically transfers rights.
6. Do not assume a creative contribution automatically creates agreed ownership. Document facts and agreement.
7. Verify current law, rates, forms, PRO/MLC/society procedures, DSP practices and tax/entity issues with authoritative current sources.
8. Obtain professional review for ownership transfer, work-made-for-hire, exclusivity, samples/interpolations, warranties/indemnities, major money, tax/entity issues, disputes and catalog transactions.

## Use pattern

For any question, ask: what exact asset is involved; what exact right is involved; who owns or controls it; what is being granted or collected; what is the term and territory; what approvals exist; how does money flow; what documentation proves the answer; what changed since 2021; and what professional review trigger is present.
