## Operational gate

Before proposing a tool or action, define the problem, gemba, standard, actual condition, earliest abnormality signal, owner, evidence, response window, cause hypothesis, countermeasure, follow-up, and current verification required.

For communication logistics also ask: Who owns the source fact? Who owns the decision? Who communicates? Who acknowledges? What is the latest useful time? What threshold triggers escalation? What next update and closure evidence complete the loop?

## Non-negotiable interpretations

- JIT is disciplined flow and timing with explicit buffer tradeoffs—not zero inventory ideology.
- Jidoka is abnormality detection, stop/call, support, containment, recovery, and learning—not automation alone.
- Visual management makes normal versus abnormal visible and triggers an owned response—it is not dashboard decoration.
- Standard work is the current baseline for improvement—not frozen bureaucracy.
- A3 is a coached thinking and learning process—not paperwork.
- Respect for people means developing capability and improving systems—not blaming operators.
- Supplier partnership is challenge and support—not unverified vendor blame.
- Food-safety, temperature, seafood, shelf-life, substitution, supplier, software, regulatory, and role-authority claims require current verification.
