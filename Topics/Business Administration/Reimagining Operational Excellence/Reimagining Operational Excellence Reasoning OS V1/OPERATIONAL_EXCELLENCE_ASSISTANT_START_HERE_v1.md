## Core thesis
Operational excellence is reimagined as an integrated operating capability: operations must support marketing promises, customer value, and stakeholder impact through QCDS and the CI-EL / PI-PM capability clusters.

## What changed in v1
This v1 repair pass reduces templated content, strengthens source traceability, makes QCDS distinct, rewrites diagnostic questions, narrows case examples to transferable lessons with cautions, and labels scorecard/dashboard/AI-agent uses as operational extensions.

## How to use the package
Start with the decision. Then identify the customer or stakeholder promise, map evidence to QCDS, choose the relevant framework and decision rule, use concrete diagnostic questions, and only then create an application output or AI workflow.

## Non-negotiable use rules
- Do not invent company-specific thresholds, formulas, policies, staffing rules, or financial targets.
- Do not use case examples as recipes.
- Do not make current claims from source-era case facts without verification.
- Do not collapse QCDS into a single vague score.
- Do not present restaurant, owner-dashboard, scorecard, or AI-agent workflows as author-prescribed methods.
- Every AI output should separate source-supported reasoning, synthesis, extension, company facts, and current facts.

## Recommended first diagnostic path
1. Use `FW-OMNIHOUSE` to locate the promise/capability gap.
2. Use `FW-QCDS-STAGES` and the four QCDS records to identify the constraint.
3. Use decision rules such as `DR-MARKETING-PROMISE-CAPABILITY`, `DR-DELIVERY-RELIABILITY-SPEED`, or `DR-COST-STAKEHOLDER-CONTRIBUTION`.
4. Use diagnostic questions under Executive, QCDS, Metrics, and AI-agent data gathering.
5. Use the relevant business application or AI workflow only after company inputs are known.
